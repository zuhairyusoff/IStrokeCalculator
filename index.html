Suggested management

For patients in the Low risk tier.

    These suggestions guide follow-up intensity and referral threshold. They are not an independent indication to start new pharmacotherapy; treatment decisions should still meet standard eligibility criteria in the cited guidelines.

    How the score was built

    The I-Stroke Score was developed from the Pulau Pinang National Diabetes Registry as part of a Doctor of Public Health thesis at Universiti Teknologi MARA. Eight predictor domains were combined into a point-based logistic model.

    Risk tiers: Low up to 14 points, Medium 15 to 19 points, High 20 points and above.
